Repairs and instrument set-up

We repair button and piano accordions and bayans and prepare instruments for sale. Every part in the catalogue is on hand, so a repair never waits for spares.

Bellows

We re-glue the leatherette and replace the card, corners, pins and the gasket between the body and the bellows. When old bellows can no longer be restored, we fit new Italian ones.

Bellows parts in the catalogue

Reeds

We clean and tune the reed plates and replace the reed valves and wax.

Reed parts

Keyboard and action

We replace keys, treble and bass buttons, springs, pallets, felt and cloth, and cure sticking keys, knocking and action noise.

Keyboard and action parts

Registers and body

We restore register switches, strap fittings, feet, celluloid and the body trim.

Registers · Body parts
